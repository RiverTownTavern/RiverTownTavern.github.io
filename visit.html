FAQ

Do I need to be a member?

Yes. River Town Tavern is members only. Ask the staff at the bar and they'll take care of you.

Do you serve food?

Absolutely. We have a full kitchen with wings, nachos, ½ lb Angus burgers, handhelds, salads, wraps and desserts. See the menu.

What nights have entertainment?

Open mic is Wednesday from 7 to 10 PM, and karaoke starts at 9 PM on Fridays. Check the events page for the current lineup.

When is steak night?

Steak night is $19.99 and the night can change, especially around holidays. Check this week's lineup or give us a call.

Can I host a party here?

Send us a message below about birthdays, get-togethers or private events and we'll get back to you.
